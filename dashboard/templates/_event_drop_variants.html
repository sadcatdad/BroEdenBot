How chances and history work

Weights are relative and do not have to total 100. A default is an ordinary weighted variant, not a fallback that overrides the others. Rare outcomes can have long random dry spells. Optional protection raises their realized frequency; the base percentages apply to ordinary weighted draws.

Expected deliveries use the last seven days' actual drop count with today's weights. Weight/mode changes, forced drops, and protection affect comparisons. History keeps the settings recorded for each drop.

Choose another enabled default before removing the current default. Deleting a used variant preserves its history.
